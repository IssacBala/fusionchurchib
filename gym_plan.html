Read first
  • Fat loss is not local. No exercise melts fat from one spot. The belly/thigh/arm shaping both of you want comes from the combined strength work + cardio below plus eating in a mild calorie deficit — the programming already targets those muscles for tone, the rest is diet and consistency.
  • Her feet: no treadmill jogging and no barefoot/standing-impact work. Cardio lives on the recumbent bike and elliptical instead. Wear a cushioned, arch-support shoe even indoors, and roll the sole on a ball or bottle for a minute or two around each session.
  • Her lower back: no heavy bent-over rows, no loaded spinal flexion, and the hyperextension bench is skipped in favor of seated/back-supported machines and floor core work (dead bug, bird-dog). If a move ever sparks sharp or radiating pain — not normal muscle fatigue — stop that exercise and see a doctor or physio. This plan doesn't replace one.
  • 5 minutes of easy bike/elliptical plus light dynamic stretching before every session; a few minutes of static stretching after.
